## Overview

CEALIS TECH STUDIO was a software engineering studio I co-founded to deliver custom software products for clients across Central Africa and the diaspora. The studio operated as a lean team, taking on high-complexity projects requiring full-stack development, product strategy, and technical leadership.

As CTO and Lead Engineer, I was responsible for all technical decisions, architecture, hiring, and direct delivery.

## Projects I Worked On

### Parametric Estimation Software (Seleri)
Designed and led the development of a construction cost estimation platform for preliminary project evaluation. The tool allows project owners and engineers to estimate costs based on parametric inputs before engaging in detailed design. Built with a Nuxt frontend, a Node.js/TypeScript API, and a structured cost database.

### ERP Engine (Xentry)
Architected and prototyped a modular ERP backend engine covering authentication, authorization, multi-tenant accounting, and pluggable business modules. Designed to serve as an internal platform for multiple SaaS products sharing common business logic.

### Client Custom Builds
Led delivery of multiple client projects ranging from internal dashboards to booking systems. Acted as both architect and lead developer, working directly with clients to refine requirements and deliver production-ready software.

Built a functioning software studio from the ground up in an emerging market context. Demonstrated that high-quality software can be built and sold from Cameroon to international standards.
